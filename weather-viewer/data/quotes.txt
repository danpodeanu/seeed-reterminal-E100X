# Weather-themed proverbs and sayings, bucketed by condition.
# 4-6 words each. Traditional English, maritime, and farmer's-almanac
# proverbs, most pre-1900 and unambiguously public domain. Any modern
# idioms are common-usage phrases with no single copyright owner.
#
# The buckets map directly onto the WMO codes rendered by the weather
# viewer, so at runtime the footer can pull a proverb that matches the
# current condition instead of picking one at random.
#
# WMO -> bucket mapping (see weather_icons::wmoToIcon):
#   0                 -> CLEAR
#   1, 2              -> PARTLY_CLOUDY
#   3                 -> OVERCAST
#   45                -> FOG
#   48                -> HAZE
#   51-55             -> DRIZZLE
#   56, 57, 66, 67    -> SLEET (freezing precipitation)
#   61-65             -> RAIN
#   71-77, 85, 86     -> SNOW
#   80-82             -> SHOWERS
#   95-99             -> THUNDERSTORM
# Plus condition-agnostic buckets: WIND, COLD, HEAT, NIGHT, UNIVERSAL.


# =========================================================================
# [CLEAR] - clear sky, sunny day (WMO 0)
# =========================================================================

A ray of sunshine on the water.
The sun is the best painter.
Nothing new under the sun.
A place in the sun.
Sunshine follows the rain.
Blue skies, calm minds.
Fair weather feeds fair thoughts.
Clear skies bring cold nights.
The sun shines even after rain.

No shadow without light.
Follow the sun, forget the clouds.

A cloudless sky, a cloudless mind.
Bright sun, bright hopes.
Where sun goes, shadows follow.
Even the sun has spots.
The morning sun never lasts a day.
Sun after storm tastes sweetest.
Under the sun, no secret keeps.
A sunny day makes the burden light.

# =========================================================================

A single cloud does not hide sun.
